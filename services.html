Services

The buildings we focus on.

Commercial, warehouse, industrial, aviation, transfer stations, and small flex. Each one is a pre-engineered metal building coordinated with Nucor and erected by SG Comp Corp.

01

Commercial

Offices, showrooms, and commercial shells on a Nucor frame. We size the bay, the glass line, and the overhead doors to the business, then erect the building.

02

Warehouse

High-bay frames, dock-ready plans, and long clear spans. We coordinate the Nucor engineering and erect the warehouse.

03

Industrial

High eaves, heavy doors, and frames that take the loads inside the plant. Tell us the process and the equipment. We size the steel around that.

04

Aviation

Engineered hangars, including wide bi-fold and sliding doors. Tell us the aircraft or the fleet and the site, and we size the frame around that.

05

Transfer stations

Clear-span steel over a deep tipping floor, tall doors for packer trucks, and a frame that stands up to the daily abuse of a transfer operation.

06

Small flex

Modest clear spans for contractors, suppliers, and light industrial tenants. Individual overhead doors, a glass entry, and a building that leases in pieces.

What a useful first note includes

  • City and state of the site
  • Use: commercial, warehouse, industrial, aviation, transfer station, or flex
  • Approximate width, length, and eave height
  • New building or an addition to one that stays open
  • Target month to be dried in or occupied
  • Drawings, if you already have them
