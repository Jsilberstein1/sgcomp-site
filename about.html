Authorized Builder

About

A Brooklyn builder on a Nucor frame.

SG Comp Corp has been active since 2015, based in Brooklyn. We design, coordinate, and erect pre-engineered metal buildings for owners, developers, and owner’s representatives anywhere Nucor can ship the steel.

What “authorized Nucor builder” means

Nucor engineers the building system and manufactures the steel, including Econiq net-zero carbon steel when a project calls for it. SG Comp Corp puts that system on your site: design coordination, delivery, and erection, with one team responsible from the first sketch to a standing frame.

Nucor’s plants cover the country. That is why a warehouse in Texas or a hangar in California is a normal SG Comp job, not a special case. Tell us the city and the use.

The steel is made in the U.S., so the schedule is tied to a domestic mill, not an overseas shipment.
