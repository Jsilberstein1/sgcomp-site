Privacy

What we do with a brief.

This site does not create an account. A quote form opens an email on your device. We do not run a mailing list from this page.

If you email info@sgcomp.us or call (716) 676-6333, we use your name, contact details, and project notes to reply about that building. We do not sell that information.

The form stays in your browser until you choose to email it. Copying a brief uses your device’s clipboard and does not send anything by itself. This site does not run analytics or tracking.

Questions about a message you already sent: call (716) 676-6333 or email info@sgcomp.us.
